Research Archive Matcher (RAM) is a secure, offline-first
research document intelligence platform. It allows you to:

  - Scan and recursively index directories of PDF papers.
  - Automatically identify structural document types (Articles, Books, TOCs).
  - Detect exact hash duplicates and potential title duplicates in your library.
  - Instantly search your index offline using full-text or field queries.
  - Search inside PDF pages, by exact phrase or by all query words,
    with similarity scores, page numbers and text snippets.
  - Open any result in a built-in PDF preview with your search
    terms highlighted, and page-by-page navigation.
  - Read scanned, image-only pages using optional OCR.
  - Align external citation lists (Excel, Word, TXT) with local PDFs.
  - Generate customized Excel matrices, Word executive summaries, and
    interactive HTML browser dashboards.

------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL: OCR FOR SCANNED DOCUMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

RAM can read scanned pages that contain no selectable text.
This feature is optional and switched off by default.

To use it, install Tesseract OCR separately:

  https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki

After installing, restart RAM and tick the option
"Enable OCR for image-only pages" on the Library Scanner tab.

RAM detects Tesseract automatically. If it is not installed,
the option stays disabled and every other feature works
normally. Only pages with no extractable text are processed,
blank pages are skipped, and your PDF files are never modified.

------------------------------------------------------------

All parsing, similarity scoring, and matching computation are
executed 100% locally on your computer, ensuring absolute
privacy for your research data. RAM never uploads your
documents anywhere.
